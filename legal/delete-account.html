How to delete your account

Option 1: From inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Gracias on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm twice — the first alert explains what will happen, the second is final.
  5. You will be signed out and redirected to the onboarding screen.

Option 2: Request via email

If you can't access the app, send an email from the address associated with your Gracias account to supermayonesso@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Gracias account". We'll process the request within 5 business days and confirm once complete.

What data is kept

No personal data is retained after deletion. Voice recordings are never stored (transcribed on-device and discarded immediately).

Retention period: When you delete via the in-app flow, data is erased immediately from our servers. When you request via email, we process the deletion within 5 business days. In both cases, data on your device is cleared when the deletion completes. No data is retained beyond these timeframes.
