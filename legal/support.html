Frequently asked questions

How do I add friends to my Circle?

Go to the Circle tab and tap the add button. Show your QR code to a friend, or scan theirs. Both of you need to agree, so nobody is added without knowing it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. All your data will be erased. See the full account deletion guide for details.

My entries aren't syncing

Make sure you're signed in with your email. Entries sync automatically when you have an internet connection.

I'm not receiving daily reminders

Check that notifications are enabled for Gracias in your iPhone's Settings app (Settings → Gracias → Notifications), and that you have a reminder time set in the app.
